# Glossary

One meaning per term. If code and prose disagree with this file, this file wins until it is
changed deliberately.

## Money and amounts

**Atomic amount** — An integer count of a token's smallest unit (e.g. `1000000` = 1 USDC at 6
decimals). Stored as a decimal string in the database, never as a float. The only representation
allowed to reach a transaction.

**Display amount** — A human decimal derived from atomic units for rendering. Never used to build
a transaction. Converting display → atomic goes through `parseDecimalToAtomic`, which throws
instead of rounding away precision.

**USD value** — A valuation for display and analytics. Always floats, always approximate, always
carries a price source and observation time. Never derived from a ticker alone.

## Portfolio

**Available** — Balance sitting in a wallet, not deployed into a strategy. Liquid.

**Deployed** — Capital committed to an open position (vault shares, supplied lending balance, LP
position). Not instantly withdrawable, and withdrawal may be subject to market liquidity.

**Total value** — `available + deployed`, both at current valuation.

**Accrued return** — Value generated by the position itself since deposit (yield, fees).

**Unrealised P&L** — `current value − cost basis`. Includes price movement, not only yield. Must
never be presented as profit already earned.

**Portfolio snapshot** — A timestamped record of balances and their valuations, with a
`dataMode` and a `dataQualityStatus`. History comes from snapshots, never from mutating a row.

## Opportunity and execution

**Opportunity** — A *research* record: a market, pool or route that might be worth using, with an
optional rate, liquidity, risk flags, source payload and observation time. Being listed means
nothing about executability.

**Quote** — A priced, time-bounded offer from a specific provider. A 1Click quote with
`dry: true` is a preview: no deposit address, nothing can move. A committed quote (`dry: false`)
returns a deposit address valid for roughly ten minutes and is the only form that can be funded.

**Executable** — A quote or call that has passed the policy engine, is unexpired, and has a valid
recipient/refund address. Executability is a property of the moment, not of the record.

**Settlement** — The provider's own state machine for a funded quote:
`PENDING_DEPOSIT → KNOWN_DEPOSIT_TX → PROCESSING → SUCCESS`, or a terminal
`REFUNDED` / `FAILED` / `INCOMPLETE_DEPOSIT`. Unknown states are surfaced verbatim, never mapped
to success.

**Atomicity (do not over-claim)** — A single 1Click swap settles one leg. A multi-leg strategy is
a sequence of independently confirmed steps with real failure and refund states in between.

## Strategies

**Yield vault** — Deposit an asset, receive a receipt/share token whose price or exchange rate
grows. 1 USDC does not equal 1 share: observed gtUSDCp share price was ≈$1.123. Share counts come
from quotes, not from a 1:1 assumption.

**Lending** — Supply an asset to a money market (e.g. Aave V3) and earn a variable supply rate
that depends on utilisation. Asset movement across chains is 1Click; the supply call itself is a
direct protocol transaction.

**Stablecoin LP** — Provide two stablecoin assets to a DEX pool and earn trading fees. Carries
depeg, concentrated-liquidity and out-of-range risk. USDT is not in the 1Click catalogue on Base
or Arbitrum, so LP scope is effectively USDC-centric.

**Cross-chain arbitrage** — Buying on one chain and selling on another for a price difference.
Executable price, not displayed price, decides profitability; both legs settle separately and the
opportunity is not atomic. Ranking by advertised spread alone is a defect.

**Tokenized equity (RWA)** — An on-chain token issued by a regulated or custody-backed provider
(e.g. Ondo, Backed, Dinari) representing economic exposure to a traditional stock, ETF, or
commodity. Distinct from native crypto and memecoins: identity is `(chainId, contractAddress)`
plus issuer/wrapper metadata, never ticker alone.

**Reference NAV** — The underlying traditional market price of an equity or ETF paired with the
market's session state (`OPEN`, `EXTENDED`, `CLOSED`) and observation timestamp. Used to measure
premium/discount against an on-chain execution quote; never treated as an executable DEX price.

**Off-hours spread** — The percentage or basis-point divergence between an on-chain DEX/RFQ
execution quote and the underlying equity's Reference NAV when traditional stock exchanges are
closed. Checked by the policy engine before permitting execution.

**Copytrading** — Mirroring a verified external wallet's non-memecoin spot rotations or DeFi
positions (`1Click Earn` vaults, Aave lending supplies, Uniswap V3 LP entries) via a single NEAR
funding and policy setup. Every mirrored leg remains subject to user guardrails and is never
presented as risk-free replication.

**Leader wallet** — Any public cross-chain address (`0x...`, `.eth`, `.sol`, `.near`) inspected
or followed for copytrading. Indexed positions remain observational until contract identity and
live execution quotes are verified.

**Price drift** — The basis-point move in an asset's executable quote price between the leader
wallet's confirmed transaction block and the follower's quote time. Checked against a deterministic
maximum cap before allowing execution.

**Cost basis** — The USD amount originally committed to a position, used to separate yield or
price return from subsequent valuation changes.

## System

**Data mode** — `sample` (fixtures, labelled), `dry_run` (live reads and live dry quotes, no funds
move), or `live` (real signing and real deposits). Displayed in the UI; never inferred silently.

**Policy engine** — Deterministic server-side code that validates a proposed allocation against
user constraints (liquidity floor, per-protocol/asset/chain/strategy caps, slippage, gas, minimum
arbitrage profit). It is the only authority that can block execution.

**Proposal** — An AI-authored, schema-validated allocation plan. It is a draft until the policy
engine validates it and the user approves it. It cannot execute anything by itself.

**Guardrail** — A user-configured constraint the policy engine enforces. Not advice, not a
suggestion, not something the model may relax.

**1Click** — NEAR Intents' swap/Earn API (`1click.chaindefuser.com`). The NEAR surface of this
product. Not a general-purpose DeFi executor: it moves assets, it does not supply to Aave or add
Uniswap liquidity.
